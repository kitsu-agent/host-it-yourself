Get it running

This is the whole setup, start to finish — set aside an afternoon. Six short steps: install Docker, grab Immich's files, point them at your storage, start it, create your account, then connect your phone. By tonight your photos can be backing up to a server you own. (Picked your hardware yet? Everything about what to run lives on the Requirements page.)

Step 1 — Install Docker

Immich runs inside containers, so Docker comes first. On most Linux machines, the official convenience script is one line:

curl -fsSL https://get.docker.com | sh

Step 2 — Download Immich's two files

Make a folder to keep everything tidy, then pull the setup files from Immich's latest release:

mkdir immich-app && cd immich-app
wget -O docker-compose.yml https://github.com/immich-app/immich/releases/latest/download/docker-compose.yml
wget -O .env https://github.com/immich-app/immich/releases/latest/download/example.env

Step 3 — Tell it where your photos live

Open the .env file and set three values:

  • UPLOAD_LOCATION — the folder where your photos should be stored (point it at your big drive).
  • DB_PASSWORD — any password, letters and numbers only.
  • TZ — your timezone, e.g. Europe/Amsterdam.

That's the only configuration you have to touch.

Step 4 — Start it

From the same folder:

docker compose up -d

The first run downloads its images — give it a minute or two.

Step 5 — Open it and make your account

In your browser, go to http://YOUR-SERVER-IP:2283 and create your admin account. That's your photo cloud, live.

Step 6 — Connect your phone

Install the Immich app from the App Store or Play Store, enter your server's address, sign in, and turn on backup. Your camera roll starts uploading in the background.

Want to go deeper? This page is the short version on purpose. The full guide — every option, every setting, and troubleshooting — lives in the repo: github.com/immich-app/immich and the docs at immich.app/docs.
