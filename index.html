Host It Yourself

Ever wondered how cloud services work or wanted to escape big tech? Let me introduce you to self-hosting. Learn how it works and have your own Google Photos up and running by today!

My own Google Photos?

Why quit Google Photos?

Your photos are the most personal thing you own — and right now they live on someone else's computer. Google Photos is convenient; that's the whole trick. But you're renting, and the rent only goes up: the free 15 GB fills fast, then it's a monthly bill that never ends. Your library gets scanned, indexed and used to build a profile of you. And the rules can change whenever a company decides they should. Your memories shouldn't work like a subscription.

What if they lived at home instead?

That's self-hosting — running software on a computer you own instead of renting someone else's. For photos, the one app to know is Immich: free, open source, and genuinely good. It looks and feels like the gallery app you already use — automatic phone backup, a timeline, smart search, albums, sharing — except the library stays with you, private by default, because there's nobody else in the loop.

Don't fear the hard part

You don't need expensive gear, a computer science degree, or a rack humming in a basement. A spare computer, a cheap drive and an afternoon are enough to start. This site takes you the whole way: what it takes, what Immich can actually do, how to install it, and how to make sure you never lose a single photo. It's a weekend project with a lifetime payoff.
